Game Mechanics

PLAY LIKE A
CHAMPION

Court Masters blends the depth of a trading card game with the pace and rules of basketball — every possession matters.

🏀

Quarter System

Play in 4 quarters like real basketball. Each quarter has unique conditions — Overtime mechanics can flip the game in an instant.

🃏

Draft Your Roster

Build a 30-card deck across 5 positions: PG, SG, SF, PF, and Center. Every position unlocks unique abilities and synergies.

⚡

Momentum Meter

Build momentum through combos and fast-break plays. Fill your Momentum Meter to unleash game-changing Hype Moves.

🏆

Team Synergies

Field 5 players with the right chemistry. Franchise bonuses activate when you field a full lineup from the same team.

🎯

Fouls & Free Throws

Strategic fouls and free-throw mechanics add pressure. Force opponents into high-stakes moments they can't escape.

🌟

All-Star Events

Monthly events with legendary cards and limited-edition cosmetics. The best players compete for the Championship Trophy.

Getting Started

HOW TO PLAY

1

Build Your Team

Choose your franchise, draft 5 starting players, and fill a 30-card deck with Plays, Tactics, and Special Moves.

2

Tip-Off

Draw 4 cards and take the first possession. Each turn is a possession — score by playing your players' attacks effectively against your opponent's defense.

3

Contest 4 Quarters

Score more points than your opponent in each quarter. Winning a quarter grants bonus passive effects. Win 3 of 4 quarters to claim victory.

4

Climb to the Finals

Win ranked matches to climb the league ladder. Reach the Finals for exclusive Championship cards and season rewards.
